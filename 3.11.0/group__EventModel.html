Detailed Description

The models of the events of one or more symbols: the order book, indexed, time series and transaction models.

Threads: dxfg_order_book_model_t, dxfg_indexed_event_model_t and dxfg_time_series_event_model_t are not thread-safe. Only their attach, detach and close functions may be called from any thread. Call any other function of an attached model, and of its lists, only from inside a listener call or from the thread of the executor that calls the listeners, or synchronize the calls. The transaction models (dxfg_indexed_tx_model_t and dxfg_time_series_tx_model_t) are thread-safe.
